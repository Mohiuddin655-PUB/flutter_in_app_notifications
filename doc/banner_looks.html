In-App Banner Looks

Each card is the package's InAppNotificationBanner while the app is open. The movement, hold, drag and actions are the package's own; everything you see can be changed from the app.

Not shown, because it has no look of its own: container replaces the whole card, content the title and body, and replyBuilder the reply field. A banner can also be any widget of your own inside InAppNotificationDrag, which still gives it the hold and the drag. Progress bars, groups, sounds and badges belong to the notification bar, not the banner.
